Questions

Which leagues have live scores?

Bundesliga, 2. Bundesliga and 3. Liga, from the OpenLigaDB community. Other leagues come from volunteer-kept files that are often days behind, and the app says so.

Can I score my own games?

Yes, in 19 sports or any other game. Your first game is free; Scoretide Pro, with a 7-day free trial, scores every game and adds the tide chart, live links and season stats.

Are these official club logos?

No. Badges are monograms drawn by Scoretide. The app is not affiliated with any league or club.
