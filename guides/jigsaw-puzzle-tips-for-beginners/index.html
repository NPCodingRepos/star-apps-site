Jigsaw puzzle tips for beginners, and how many edge pieces

A simple method for any jigsaw: edges first, then colour and shape. Plus how many edge pieces a 500 or 1000 piece puzzle has, and what size to start with.

Updated 2026-10-22

Most people solve their first jigsaw the same way: pick up a piece, look for where it goes, put it down, repeat. It works, but it is slow. A few habits that regular puzzlers use make it faster and more relaxing, on a table or on a phone.

Step by step

  1. Turn every piece picture side up. It feels like a chore, but it is the quickest way to see what you have.
  2. Find the edge pieces (one straight side) and the four corners (two straight sides). Build the frame first: it fixes the size of the picture and gives every other piece something to grow from.
  3. Sort the rest by what you can see: a colour, a texture, writing, a face, a horizon. Keep a small pile or area for each.
  4. Build the distinctive parts first and place them inside the frame, using the picture as your map.
  5. Leave the hard areas, like plain sky or deep shadow, for last. By then fewer pieces are left, and you can sort them by shape: count the tabs (knobs) and blanks (holes) on each piece.
  6. Take breaks. Fresh eyes find the piece you have been looking past for ten minutes.

How many edge pieces does a puzzle have?

It depends on the grid the puzzle is cut in, not only on the piece count. For a puzzle cut in R rows and C columns, the number of edge pieces, corners included, is 2 x (R + C) - 4. The number on the box can be rounded, because the grid has to fit the shape of the picture.

A long, thin picture has more edge pieces than a squarer one with the same count: a 1000-piece puzzle cut 50 x 20 has 136. So if your frame is finished and you have counted far fewer than about 120 edge pieces in a 1000-piece puzzle, a few are probably still in the box.

What size to start with

There is no official scale. This is our suggestion for adults who are new to jigsaws:

PiecesGood for
16 to 100Learning the method, or a quick puzzle on a phone
150 to 300A first puzzle on the table: bigger pieces, quick to finish
500The classic size, once the method feels natural
1000 and moreWhen you enjoy long sessions and big, detailed pictures

The picture matters as much as the count. A busy painting full of colour is easier than a calm one with a big plain sky, even with the same number of pieces.

Questions

How many edge pieces are in a 500 piece puzzle?

Usually between 85 and 90, depending on the grid. A puzzle cut 25 x 20 has 86 edge pieces, corners included.

How many edge pieces are in a 1000 piece puzzle?

Usually between about 120 and 140. A puzzle cut 40 x 25 has 126; a longer, thinner one has more, for example 136 for 50 x 20.

What is the fastest way to do a jigsaw puzzle?

Edges first, then sort by colour and detail, build the distinctive areas, and leave plain areas for the end, where sorting by piece shape helps most.
