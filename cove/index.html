What it does

Sudoku Garden

4x4 and 6x6 sudoku with fruit pictures, 9x9 with numbers. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Bubble Math

Pop the right answer: counting, adding, taking away, times tables and dividing.

Memory, mazes and patterns

Memory Shells, Maze Run, Pattern Parade, Fish Count, Shadow Shapes and Puzzle Pals.

Grows with your child

Levels start at the right point for ages 3-5, 6-8 or 9-11, and every instruction is spoken aloud.

Stars and stickers

Stars for every round and a sticker book of sea friends to fill.

Made for families

No ads, no accounts, no tracking. Purchases and settings sit behind a parent check, with progress for each child.

Questions

Is my child's data collected?

No. Nothing your child does leaves the device. There are no ads and no analytics.

Is there a free version?

Yes: Sudoku Garden, Bubble Math and Memory Shells with their first levels. The Family Pass opens all 8 games for up to 4 children.

Can my child buy something by accident?

No. Purchases and settings are behind a parent check that young children cannot pass.
