What it does

A free daily masterpiece

Every day a new painting or print, free for everyone, with its title, artist, date and source like a museum label.

Real 3D pieces

Classic jigsaw shapes with light, shade and a drop shadow; pieces click into place with a sound and a gentle buzz.

The whole museum

Search The Met's Open Access collection for any public-domain painting and play it as a puzzle.

16 to 600 pieces

Classic, soft and square-tab cuts, an optional rotation mode, edge and colour sorting, zoom and hints.

Your photos

Turn any photo into a puzzle. It stays on your phone.

Your gallery wall

Every finished puzzle hangs framed on your own wall, with your time, best times and a daily streak.

Questions

Are the paintings licensed?

Yes. They are public-domain works from The Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access program (CC0) and photographs by NASA, credited on every puzzle.

Is there a free version?

Yes: the daily masterpiece, the Starter Room, your own photos, up to 100 pieces and one museum puzzle a day. The Atelier Pass opens everything.

Are there ads?

No, never. There is no account and no tracking.
